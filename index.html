Ground classification / 2023

Reading lithology from machine logs

One ground class accounted for three quarters of the observations. We grouped similar machine records before training LightGBM to recognise the less common ground types.

Read the lithology study

Tool wear / 2022

Estimating disc-cutter life

Machine records from 723 tunnel rings and wear measurements from four inspections let us estimate how much ground a disc cutter can excavate per millimetre of wear.

Research method

Connect what the machine records to what engineers find underground.

My studies pair thrust, torque and pressure readings with geological logs or cutter inspections. Those field observations give the models a physical reference for interpreting changes during excavation.
